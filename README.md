# Superstore Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, shipping trends, and regional business growth across the United States.

This dashboard transforms raw retail data into actionable insights through dynamic visualizations and KPI-driven analytics, enabling businesses to monitor performance, identify profitable areas, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity performance
- Identify high-performing product categories and sub-categories
- Track monthly sales and profit trends
- Evaluate regional and state-wise business performance
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Analyze shipping mode efficiency and sales distribution

---

## Technology Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated measures and KPI creation
- **Data Modeling** – Relationship management and cross-filtering
- **File Formats** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews

---

## Data Source

The dataset contains retail sales transaction data including:

- Orders and sales records
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Customer segments
- Shipping modes
- City and state information
- Profit and quantity metrics

The data supports multidimensional analysis across products, customers, and regions.

## Dashboard Analysis

### KPI Summary

The dashboard provides a quick overview of business performance:

- **Total Sales:** 2.30M
- **Total Quantity:** 38K
- **Total Profit:** 286.40K

These KPIs help monitor overall operational and financial performance.

---

### Sales by Segment

A donut chart analyzes sales contribution across customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

#### Insights
- Consumer segment generates the highest sales contribution
- Corporate customers maintain a significant share of revenue
- Helps businesses understand target customer groups

---

### Category-wise Sales Analysis

A bar chart compares sales across major categories:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

#### Insights
- Technology category leads overall sales
- Furniture and Office Supplies maintain balanced contributions
- Supports category-level performance evaluation

---

### Monthly Sales and Profit Trends

Line charts visualize Year-over-Year monthly sales and profit performance for 2018 and 2019.

#### Insights
- Sales show strong growth during the final months of the year
- Profit trends fluctuate across months, highlighting seasonal business patterns
- Helps identify high-performing periods and business cycles

---

### Regional and State-wise Analysis

The dashboard includes:
- Regional filters (Central, East, South, West)
- Interactive map visualization for state-level sales analysis

#### Insights
- Sales are distributed across multiple states with strong regional concentration
- Geographic analysis supports regional expansion and market strategy planning

---

### Sales by City

A donut chart displays top-performing cities based on sales contribution.

#### Insights
- New York contributes the highest city-level sales
- Los Angeles and Seattle also generate strong revenue
- Helps identify high-value urban markets

---

### Shipping Mode Analysis

A bar chart compares sales across shipping methods:

- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day

#### Insights
- Standard Class dominates total sales shipments
- Same Day delivery has the lowest contribution
- Useful for logistics and operational planning

---

### Sales by Sub-Category

A horizontal bar chart analyzes top-performing sub-categories:

- Phones
- Chairs
- Binders

#### Insights
- Phones generate the highest sales revenue
- Chairs maintain strong performance in furniture
- Supports product-level profitability analysis

---

## Business Impact

This dashboard helps businesses:
